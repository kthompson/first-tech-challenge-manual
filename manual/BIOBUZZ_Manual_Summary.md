## 1. Introduction

- **FIRST** = "For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology." Mission: "give young people the skills, confidence, and resilience to build a better world."
- **What makes FTC distinct:** Scale (classroom-sized field/safe robots), Skill Level (scaffolded from classroom to championship), Complexity (Starter Kit or full custom fabrication), Flexibility (season runs September–March, capstone events April–July), Student Ownership.
- **Gracious Professionalism®** (coined by Dr. Woodie Flowers) and **Coopertition®** (cooperating even with direct competitors) are the foundational philosophies. **Six Core Values:** Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, Fun.
- **Manual philosophy shift for 2026-2027:** rules now emphasize the "spirit of the rule" and "intended gameplay" rather than exhaustively covering every scenario — volunteers are empowered to make good-faith judgment calls (e.g., watching for strategic blocking patterns rather than recording every gate-zone entry).
- **Framework of Behaviors** (four pillars): Gracious Professionalism & Core Values, FIRST Code of Conduct, Competition Integrity Contract (CIC), and the Competition Rules (Manual/Team Updates/Q&A).
- **Competition Integrity Contract (CIC)** — new this season, a certified team pledge in two parts:
  - **Sporting Ethics Code** (mandatory): Don't Cheat; Always Behave with Integrity; Play the Game as Intended (no loophole-hunting; resolve ambiguity via rule intent); Know Your Team Doesn't Enforce the Rules (report concerns to staff, don't self-police).
  - **Behavior Guidelines** (aspirational): Focus on Journey Before Destination; Respect & Good Sportsmanship; Help Each Other; Take the High Road; Respect Property; Build a Positive Safety Culture; Make Healthy Choices; We're All on the Same Team.
  - Escalation path: LRI/Head Referee/FTA for mitigation → Event Director/FIRST HQ for serious violations → possible sanction, disqualification, suspension, or removal.
- **Accessibility:** FIRST commits to "STEM for Everyone™"; accommodations must be requested from local leadership before the event.
- **Manual conventions:** it's the "source of truth" over other documents; blue text = cross-links; ALL CAPS = glossary terms; orange boxes = non-binding guidance (rule text always wins in a conflict); metric conversions are reference-only (imperial governs); rule letters map to sections — **I**=Eligibility/Inspection, **E**=Event Rules, **A**=Awards, **G**=Game Rules, **R**=Robot Construction, **T**=Tournament, **L**=League Play, **C**=Championship. "Evergreen" rules (recurring every season) get bold green asterisked headlines.
- **Team Updates** post every Thursday from Kickoff until 2 weeks before Championship (additions in yellow, deletions struck through).
- **Q&A system opens September 28, 2026, 12:00 p.m. ET**, via Lead Coach dashboard access; moderators answer Monday–Thursday (closing 5:00 p.m. ET Thursday); Referees/Inspectors remain the ultimate on-site rule authority.

## 3. Competition Eligibility and Inspection (I)

- **Team eligibility (I101):** must be registered and "competition ready" — North America requires FIRST dashboard registration, paid annual fee, 2 adults in Lead Coach 1/2 roles with Youth Protection Program (YPP) screening, and all youth registered.
- **Check-in (I102):** an adult must check in **no later than 45 minutes before Qualification Matches**, with at least 1 student present.
- **Responsible adult (I103):** at least 1 (preferably 2) responsible adult must be present for the whole event.
- **Awards eligibility:** requires participating in an Initial Interview (A203); some awards also require a PORTFOLIO.
- **Match/Inspection eligibility:** robots must pass INSPECTOR review before Qualification/Playoff matches. The **LRI (Lead Robot Inspector)** has final authority on construction-rule eligibility. Inspection is explicitly **"not comprehensive"** — teams must follow the spirit of the Robot Construction Rules even where inspectors don't check everything; strategic circumvention violates the CIC.
- **Bring everything (I301):** teams must present the complete robot, operator console, and all components (including decorative parts) that will be used; un-inspected components can't be added without re-inspection.
- **Re-inspection (I303/I304):** required for most robot changes; exceptions include fastener changes, labeling, SIGN changes, code revisions, identical-part swaps. Teams may not exploit re-inspection to dodge other rules.

## 4. Advancement

- Teams can only advance from events in their **HOME REGION**.
- **Tournament ladder:** Qualifying Tournament (QT) or League Tournament (LT) → optional Super Qualifying Tournament (SQT, once per team per season) → Regional Championship (RCMP) → FIRST Championship (FCMP) and/or FIRST Premier Events (FPE). New/emerging regions may use Pilot events feeding directly to Championship/Premier Events.
- **Advancement points (Table 4-1):**
  - Qualification Phase Performance: normal distribution, **2–16 points**, via an inverse-error-function formula (rank R, team count N, static α = 1.07).
  - Alliance Lead: 21 − alliance lead number (e.g., 18 pts for Alliance #3 lead).
  - Draft Order Acceptance: 21 − draft position number.
  - Playoff Advancement: **40 / 20 / 10 / 5** points for 1st (Winners) / 2nd (Finalists) / 3rd / 4th.
  - Team Judged Awards: **60/30/15** for Inspire Award 1st/2nd/3rd; **12/6/3** for all other awards' 1st/2nd/3rd.
- **Tiebreakers (Table 4-2, 10 levels):** Total Advancement Points → Judged Award Points → Playoff Points → Alliance Selection Points → Qualification Performance Points → average Qual match points (excl. fouls) → average Qual AUTO points → highest/2nd-highest individual Qual score → random selection.
- **Regional allocation:** Program Delivery Partners set/publish minimum regional advancement numbers before Alliance Selection begins. FIRST HQ determines Championship/Premier Event slots based on registration counts before the **November 17, 2026** cutoff.

## 5. Event Rules (E)

Applies from public schedule start through venue departure. **Universal Violation Note:** any Event Rule violation → VERBAL WARNING first; egregious/repeat violations escalate further.

- **General Rules (E101–E118), all Evergreen:**
  - Certified safety glasses required (10-min grace period at venue opening), closed-toe shoes, hair/clothing safety.
  - **"Be Respectful" (E102):** bans "weaponizing Gracious Professionalism" (accusing others of "not being GP"); bans assault, threats, harassment, bullying, insulting, yelling.
  - Children under 12 need adult supervision in pits.
  - Fabrication only in designated areas.
  - Prohibited items: skateboards, hoverboards, drones, gas tanks, noisemakers, fast-flashing lights.
  - No unapproved sales, no loud music/live bands.
  - Banner limits (**≤25 ft² outside pits**), flag/flagpole size and weight limits.
  - No firearms/weapons (including realistic props).
  - No unconsented recording of others.
  - **Only 1 robot per team per event; only 1 concurrent event per team (E117).**
  - No seat-saving (E118).
- **Wireless Rules:** no team-run Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/2.4-5GHz devices at the venue; no interfering with or connecting to others' networks without permission.
- **Pits:** typically **10ft × 10ft × 10ft** per team; power may be unavailable overnight. No open flames/welding/brazing (soldering iron OK only); only small benchtop machinery (3D printers, small band saws, desktop CNC — no full-size floor equipment); no heating/cooling robot parts for competitive advantage; **battery charging capped at 3-amp average channel current** with polarized connectors (no alligator clips).
- **Robot Carts:** must be easy to control, no bystander risk, **fit through a standard 30-inch door**, stay in the pit when unused, no powered propulsion or sound devices.
- **Ceremonies:** no power tools/loud tools/shouting during non-playoff ceremonies; **max 5 team members** in pits during ceremonies; peaceful behavior during anthems (right to silently abstain).

## 6. Awards (A)

- Judged by **JUDGES** (evaluate against criteria) supervised by a **JUDGE Advisor** (trains/supervises, doesn't pick winners). No awards at League Meets.
- **Three judged categories:** Machine, Creativity, and Innovation (MCI); Team Attributes (TA); Documentation — plus the standalone Judges' Choice Award and the top overall **Inspire Award**.
- **Judging season starts January 1, 2026** — judges can't consider anything from before that date, outside the event, from social media, or from match performance (unless specifically an award criterion).
- **Initial Interview** (renamed from "Structured Interview"): 3 formats (scheduled in-person, unscheduled pit, or remote video); **at least 10 minutes**, first 5 minutes reserved for uninterrupted team presentation, remainder is Q&A. The Judge Advisor selects 2 standard questions (1 MCI-focused, 1 TA-focused) asked of every team. 1 optional silent adult observer permitted; translator accommodation available with advance request. No recording permitted.
- **PORTFOLIO (A201):** cover page + **max 15 pages**; US Letter or A4 only; **<15MB** if digital; content restricted to on/after **January 1, 2026**; minimize personal info (first name + last initial only); AI-assisted portfolios allowed with a credit footnote.
- **Award scaling by event size (Table 6-1):** 4-10 teams get 1st place only for most awards; larger events add 2nd/3rd place tiers up to 41-64 teams.
- Teams can win **only one team judged award per event** (A215); Inspire 1st Place can only be won **once per season** at Qualifying/League Tournaments (A214).
- **Named awards:** Inspire (top overall, requires portfolio), Think (documentation/engineering process), Connect / Reach / Sustain (Team Attributes — professional development, outreach, sustainability), Innovate (sponsored by RTX, creative mechanism design) / Control (external-feedback control systems) / Design (industrial design), Judges' Choice.
- **Alliance Awards:** Winning Alliance and Finalist Alliance (given to Playoff finalists).
- **Individual Awards:** FIRST Leadership Award (renamed from Dean's List Award); Compass Award (recognizes an adult mentor via a 40–60 second student-produced video, offered at Regional Championship level and above).
- **Project-Based Global Awards:** judged once per season, independent of event advancement — details "coming soon."

## 12. ROBOT Construction Rules (R) — the most developed technical chapter

- **Core definitions:** **COMPONENT** (smallest non-disassemblable part) vs. **MECHANISM** (assembly of components, reversibly disassemblable); **COTS** = standard off-the-shelf part available to all teams from a legitimate **VENDOR**; **FABRICATED ITEM** = anything altered/built/machined toward final robot form.
- **General Design:**
  - Robot and its **MAJOR MECHANISMS** must be built by the team (R101).
  - **Starting size: 18in × 18in × 18in cube (R102)**; robot must be stationary and self-contained at start.
  - **No overall weight limit (R104)**, though field/battery/transport considerations still apply.
  - Robots must remain one assembly; may expand post-start (specific size constraints TBD at Kickoff) (R105).
- **Fair Play & Damage Prevention:** no arena-damaging materials (sand, kitty litter, liquids, graphite powder); no vision-blocking devices, distracting audio mimicking match sounds, sensor-jamming devices (including fake AprilTag imagery), flammable/hazardous materials, floor-grabbing suction mechanisms.
- **Fabrication:** purpose-built COTS major mechanisms are banned (except COTS drive chassis and official StarterBot mechanisms); COTS components are generally limited to a **single degree of freedom** (with named exceptions: ratchets, omni/mecanum wheels, dead-wheel odometry, misalignment couplers, variable-angle linkages); pre-Kickoff custom software/designs are reusable.
- **ROBOT SIGN:** minimum 2 signs on different robot faces, **≥6.5in wide × ≥2.5in tall**, with a solid red/blue alliance-color rectangle and ~2.25in-tall white team-number digits; not power-illuminated.
- **Motors & Actuators:**
  - Legal motor list includes named brands (AndyMark, goBILDA, REV, Studica, TETRIX, WATTOS, etc.) — all 12V DC.
  - Servo limits: Servo class ≤8W mechanical output @6V; Linear Servo class ≤1A stall current @6V.
  - **Max 8 motors and 8 servos total.**
  - Actuators must run through approved power-regulating devices (2 motors/servos per port on most devices).
  - Relays, electromagnets, and electrical solenoid actuators are prohibited.
- **Power Distribution:**
  - Exactly **1 main 12V NiMH battery** from an approved list (AndyMark, goBILDA, Modern Robotics, REV, Studica, TETRIX, WATTOS).
  - Exactly 1 main power switch controlling all battery power, from an approved list, accessible and away from pinch hazards.
  - Custom circuits capped at **5V regulated output** unless powering LEDs only.
  - Minimum wire gauges: **18 AWG** for main/motor power, **22 AWG** for PWM/servo/LED circuits, **28 AWG** for signal-level circuits.
  - Standard wire color coding for positive/negative power leads.
- **Control, Command & Signals:**
  - Single **ROBOT CONTROLLER**: REV Control Hub (officially supported) or a smartphone + REV Expansion Hub, plus optionally 1 more Expansion Hub.
  - No modifying coprocessor software except approved vision coprocessors (currently only **Limelight 3A** is supported).
  - No non-official wireless communication to/from the robot; explicitly **bans FTC Dashboard, FTControl Panels**, and similar third-party streaming tools; only the FTC Driver Station App may stream control/debug/telemetry data.
  - Device naming convention: `<team#>-RC` / `<team#>-DS`.
  - USB ports restricted to webcams/vision sensors, USB hubs, and the REV Expansion Hub; only single-image-sensor UVC webcams or approved vision coprocessors are allowed (no stereoscopic cameras).
  - Lasers only permitted as part of a sensor, IEC/EN 60825-1 Class I, non-visible spectrum.
  - Required device settings: Wi-Fi password changed from default, Airplane Mode on smartphones, Bluetooth disabled, stale Wi-Fi Direct connections removed.
- **Pneumatic Systems & Airflow Devices:** essentially banned — no pneumatic actuators, high-speed blowers, or vacuums; only sealed, pre-charged COTS closed-air systems (e.g., gas shocks) allowed; no solenoid-actuated stored pressure; no pressure/vacuum generation; cooling fans integral to COTS computing devices are the only exception for airflow devices. High-speed flywheels/rollers for scoring elements are not considered "airflow devices."
- **Operator Console:**
  - Single approved Android **Driver Station** device: REV Driver Hub (officially supported) or another Android device.
  - Max size **3ft wide × 1ft 6in deep × 2ft tall**, excluding driver-worn/held items.
  - No wireless communication within the console other than the official Robot Controller↔Driver Station link.

## 14. League Play Tournaments (L)

- Leagues run multiple League Meet (LM) events over weeks/months; **minimum 10 League Meet matches** per team across the league; each League Meet has **5–6 Qualification Matches per team**, no Playoffs, no award judging.
- Yellow/Red Card and Verbal Warning records clear at the end of each League Meet.
- Teams may join **only 1 League per season** and participate in only 1 League Tournament.
- League Tournament rankings blend each team's **top 10 League Meet matches** with League Tournament matches; teams with fewer than 10 League Meet matches get zero for missing slots.

## 16. Glossary (selected key terms)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **ROBOT** | Electromechanical assembly (power, comms, control, movement) built by the team to play the game |
| **COMPONENT** | Smallest part that can't be disassembled without damage or loss of function |
| **MECHANISM** | Assembly of components with specific functionality, reversibly disassemblable |
| **MAJOR MECHANISM** | Component/mechanism group addressing at least one game challenge |
| **ALLIANCE** | Cooperative of 2 FTC teams |
| **ARENA** | All game infrastructure: field, scoring elements, queue/media areas, control equipment |
| **CHASSIS** | The robot's movement-enabling major mechanism |
| **COTS** | Standard off-the-shelf part commonly available to all teams from a Vendor |
| **VENDOR** | Legitimate business (own Tax ID, not team-owned, adequate stock, open to all teams) |
| **HOME REGION** | The region a team is assigned to and can only advance from |
| **INSPECTOR / LRI** | Volunteer(s) who assess robot/parts legality; LRI has final authority |
| **REFEREE** | Certified official enforcing game rules |
| **JUDGE / JUDGE Advisor** | Evaluate teams for awards / train and supervise judges |
| **PORTFOLIO** | Judging document, max 15 pages, per A201 |
| **STARTING CONFIGURATION** | Physical robot configuration at match start (≤18in cube) |
| **ROBOT CONTROLLER** | REV Control Hub or smartphone + REV Expansion Hub running the control app |
| **OPERATOR CONSOLE** | Components/mechanisms the Drive Team uses to command the robot |
| **ROBOT SIGN** | Required team-number/alliance-color identifier plate |
| **STUDENT** | Person not yet through secondary school as of September 1st |
| **TILE** | One of 36 interlocking foam field-floor tiles |
| **VERBAL WARNING** | Lowest-tier violation consequence issued by staff/Head Referee |
| **CIC** | Competition Integrity Contract — the team behavioral pledge (Sporting Ethics Code + Behavior Guidelines) |
